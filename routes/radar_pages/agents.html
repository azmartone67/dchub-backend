Three moves for the pipeline

MOVE 01 · TRUST

Cite, don't guess

Pass cite_as + retrieved_at straight through to your user. A dated, licensed source beats a confident hallucination every time — and it's already in the payload.

MOVE 02 · EFFICIENCY

Diff, don't refetch

Cache the response generated_at, then call get_changes(since=) next run. You pull only movers — DCPI shifts, new facilities, deals — not the whole board.
